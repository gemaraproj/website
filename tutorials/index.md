Tutorials are lessons. Each one walks you through a small, complete piece of work from start to finish, so you can learn Gemara by doing it rather than reading about it. You don't need to know Gemara first.

- **[Write and validate your first control catalog](first-control-catalog/)**: write a two-control catalog in YAML, check it against the Gemara schema and fix a validation error. About 10 minutes.

Already know what you want to build? The [how-to guides](../how-to/) cover each kind of artifact step by step. To understand the ideas behind Gemara, start with [The Gemara model](../model/).
